WorkFlow

An automated scheduling assistant, powered by the Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) and Gemini, that manages your Google Calendar and Google Tasks through natural conversation.

What it does: WorkFlow lets you create, search, update, and delete calendar events and tasks by chatting in plain language ("move my 3pm to Friday", "what's on my calendar tomorrow"). It connects to your Google Calendar and Google Tasks using Google Sign-In, and uses Gemini to interpret your requests.
